Podcast Shadowing

Listen 10 minutes a day and repeat after the speaker (this is called "shadowing"). We'll choose one podcast episode together — about an hour long — and finish it as a team, every 1–2 weeks.

How to Practice
  • Speak out loud. Don't just listen — repeat what you hear, right after the speaker says it.
  • Keep going. If you hear a word you don't know, don't stop. You can look it up later.
  • Repeat hard parts. If a sentence is tricky, play it again 2–3 times before moving on.
  • Copy the style. Try to sound like the speaker — fast or slow, calm or excited.

English Journaling

Write for 5 minutes in English, in your notebook — morning or night, whatever you prefer. Write anything you're thinking about.

How to Practice
  • Don't worry about mistakes. The goal is to finish 5 minutes of writing, not to write perfectly.
  • Stuck on a word? Write "I don't know how to say ___" in English and keep going. It's okay to leave gaps.
  • Use one notebook. Keep every entry in the same place, so you can look back and see your progress.
  • Check with AI. After you finish, ask an AI to only point out your mistakes — then fix just those parts.
