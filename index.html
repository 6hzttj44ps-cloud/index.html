Weekly Practice

Podcast Shadowing

Listen 10 minutes on Sunday and Tuesday and repeat after the speaker (this is called “shadowing”). We’ll choose one podcast episode together — about an hour long — and finish it as a team.

How to Practice
  • Speak out loud. Don’t just listen — repeat what you hear, right after the speaker says it.
  • Keep going. If you hear a word you don’t know, don’t stop. Look it up later.
  • Repeat hard parts. Play tricky sentences 2–3 times before moving on.
  • Copy the style. Try to sound like the speaker — fast or slow, calm or excited.

English Journaling

Write for 5 minutes in English on Sunday and Tuesday, in your notebook — morning or night, whatever you prefer. Write anything you’re thinking about.

How to Practice
  • Don’t worry about mistakes. The goal is to finish 5 minutes of writing, not to write perfectly.
  • Stuck on a word? Write “I don’t know how to say ___” in English and keep going.
  • Use one notebook. Keep every entry in the same place, so you can look back and see your progress.
  • Check with AI. After you finish, ask an AI to only point out your mistakes — then fix just those parts.

Review Class Feedback

Spend 10 minutes twice a day after each class reviewing the feedback you received. Write down your mistakes and say the corrected sentences out loud. Use the Sunday and Tuesday sessions for this practice.

How to Practice
  • Review your feedback. Look at the mistakes from class while they are still fresh.
  • Write it down. Record the mistake and the corrected version in your notebook.
  • Say it out loud. Repeat each corrected sentence until it feels natural.
